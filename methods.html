01. INTRINSIC Income Approach

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)

Calculates the present value of expected future cash flows using WACC as the discount rate to capture time value of money.

DCF = ∑ [ CFt / (1 + r)t ] + PV(TV)
Pros:

Grounded in fundamental cash generation independent of market hype.

Cons:

Highly sensitive to long-term discount rates and growth assumptions.

02. RELATIVE Public Market

Comparable Company Analysis

Values a business by applying valuation multiples (P/E, P/S, EV/EBITDA) derived from similar publicly traded companies.

Multiple = Market Price / Financial Metric
Pros:

Reflects current, real-time public market valuations and sentiment.

Cons:

Struggles with unique startups or niche firms lacking direct peers.

03. TRANSACTIONAL M&A Benchmark

Precedent Transactions Analysis

Examines actual takeover valuation metrics from historical corporate acquisitions of similar target companies.

Acquisition Multiple = Deal Price / Target Metric
Pros:

Includes real acquisition control premiums paid by strategic buyers.

Cons:

Past deal data can be scarce, private, or quickly outdated.

04. ASSET-BASED Balance Sheet

Asset-Based Valuation

Calculates company worth based strictly on the net fair market value of its tangible and intangible assets minus liabilities.

Asset Value = Fair Value Assets - Liabilities
Pros:

Provides a concrete baseline for asset-heavy capital companies.

Cons:

Ignores future earning power and brand goodwill value.

05. MULTIPLES Operating Profit

Earnings Multiples

Uses standard multiples of operational earnings or EV/EBITDA to evaluate target profitability quickly against industry peers.

EV / EBITDA = Enterprise Value / EBITDA
Pros:

Straightforward, fast industry rule-of-thumb comparison.

Cons:

Requires verified, sustainable, non-manipulated EBITDA figures.

06. DISTRESSED Exit / Bankruptcy

Liquidation Valuation

Estimates cash value generated if a business ceases operations immediately, sells off assets at discount, and repays debt.

Liquidation = Fire-Sale Asset Value - Debt
Pros:

Establishes the absolute floor valuation for troubled firms.

Cons:

Assumes complete loss of ongoing operational business value.

3. Multi-Method Valuation Comparator

Compare target company valuation output across all six frameworks simultaneously to establish a comprehensive valuation range.

Valuation Range Analysis

Corporate finance best practices dictate using a "football field" range chart rather than relying on a single valuation point.

  • • DCF & Precedent Transactions usually establish the upper valuation boundary due to optimistic growth and acquisition premiums.
  • • Public Multiples (CCA) present an average market benchmark for ongoing operations.
  • • Asset-Based & Liquidation set the strict downward protection floor in scenario planning.
💡 Tip: Use this range during buyer-seller M&A price negotiations to defend key metrics.
