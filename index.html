Valuation frameworks

Six ways to examine business value.

Different methods answer different questions. A robust valuation may consider more than one approach and reconcile the results.

01 / INCOME

Discounted Cash Flow

PV of forecast FCF + PV of TV

Estimates intrinsic enterprise value by discounting expected future cash flows.

02 / MARKET

Comparable Companies

Enterprise value ÷ operating metric

Uses trading multiples from comparable listed businesses as a market reference.

03 / MARKET

Precedent Transactions

Transaction value ÷ financial metric

Considers prices paid in relevant acquisitions, including deal-specific factors.

04 / ASSET

Asset-Based Valuation

Fair value of assets − liabilities

Examines the fair value of assets and obligations, with adjustments where appropriate.

05 / EARNINGS

Earnings Multiples

EBITDA × selected market multiple

Applies a relevant earnings multiple, informed by sector, scale and risk.

06 / DOWNSIDE

Liquidation Valuation

Realizable asset proceeds − costs − liabilities

Considers potential net proceeds if assets are sold and obligations settled.
